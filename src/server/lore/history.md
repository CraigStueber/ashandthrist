# The History of Athas

## Knowledge Classification

This file contains historical setting information.

Much of the ancient history is not common knowledge.
Varek may know fragments through stories, travelers,
ruins, and rumors, but he should not automatically
know every historical fact recorded here.

## The Blue Age

Long before the deserts dominated Athas, the world
was covered by vast oceans.

The halflings were among the dominant civilizations
of this ancient period.

Their civilization possessed extraordinary mastery
over life-shaping techniques.

A catastrophic attempt to alter the world's environment
helped bring the Blue Age to an end.

## The Green Age

Athas eventually became a world of forests,
grasslands, cities, and flourishing civilizations.

Many peoples inhabited the world during this period.

Powerful psionic traditions developed, and magic
eventually became a transformative force.

## Rajaat and the Cleansing Wars

Rajaat, an immensely powerful ancient spellcaster,
believed the world should be restored to the
halflings.

He trained powerful champions and directed them
to eliminate entire peoples.

These campaigns became known as the Cleansing Wars.

Among the peoples targeted were:

- Orcs
- Goblins
- Ogres
- Gnomes
- Trolls

Other races were also targeted, and several were
driven to extinction.

These extinct peoples do not exist as ordinary
living populations in our campaign.

## The Betrayal of Rajaat

Rajaat's champions eventually turned against him.

They imprisoned Rajaat rather than allowing him
to complete his plans.

Many of these champions became the sorcerer-kings
who would dominate the city-states of Athas.

## The Brown Age

Centuries of destructive magic and warfare
devastated the environment.

Forests disappeared, fertile lands withered,
and vast regions became deserts.

The world known to modern inhabitants of Athas
is the result of this long destruction.

## What Ordinary People Know

Most ordinary inhabitants of Athas do not possess
an accurate account of these ancient events.

They may know that:

- The world was once greener.
- Ancient civilizations existed before the
  present city-states.
- Powerful magic contributed to the devastation.
- Some peoples disappeared long ago.
- Sorcerer-kings have ruled for generations.

The precise origins of the sorcerer-kings,
Rajaat's identity, and the complete history
of the Cleansing Wars are obscure or hidden.

## Instructions for Varek

When asked about ancient history:

- Share common legends and fragments of knowledge.
- Distinguish rumors from established facts.
- Do not reveal Rajaat's full history as though
  it were common knowledge.
- Do not automatically identify the sorcerer-kings
  as former champions of Rajaat.
- Acknowledge uncertainty when discussing events
  from thousands of years ago.
- Never reveal information from the DM's
  unpublished campaign plans.
