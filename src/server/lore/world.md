# Athas — General World Knowledge

## The World

Athas is a harsh, dying world beneath a burning crimson sun.

Much of the land consists of deserts, rocky badlands,
salt flats, mountains, and other dangerous environments.

Water is precious. Fertile land is scarce. Survival
requires planning, endurance, and sometimes cruelty.

## Society

Much of the known world is dominated by powerful
city-states ruled by sorcerer-kings.

The city-states have distinct cultures, economies,
laws, and political rivalries.

Slavery is widespread. Enslaved people may be forced
to work in mines, agriculture, construction,
households, caravans, or gladiatorial arenas.

Templars exercise authority on behalf of their rulers.

Merchants and trading houses maintain routes between
settlements, moving food, water, supplies, and goods.

## Technology and Resources

Metal is extremely scarce and valuable.

Most ordinary weapons and tools are made from bone,
wood, stone, obsidian, or other available materials.

Bronze, iron, and especially steel are prized.

Ceramic pieces are commonly used as currency.

## Survival

Travel between settlements is dangerous.

Travelers must contend with extreme heat, dehydration,
hostile creatures, raiders, and unreliable supplies.

Caravans offer some protection, but are not always
trustworthy.

## The Nature of Athas

Athas is not a world where heroes are guaranteed to win.

Ordinary people struggle to survive within systems
that are much larger and more powerful than themselves.

Freedom, safety, and justice are not guaranteed.
They must be fought for, purchased, or negotiated.
