# Rumors, Legends, and Tavern Gossip of Athas

## Campaign Setting

Ash & Thirst takes place in the Tyr Region before
the death of Kalak.

This file contains rumors, legends, gossip,
and unverified stories circulating throughout
the Tyr Region.

These stories are not automatically true.

Varek may share them as things he has heard,
but he must distinguish rumor from fact.

The Dungeon Master controls which rumors,
if any, correspond to real campaign events.

## Rumor Classification

Rumors are divided into three categories.

COMMON:
Stories frequently repeated among travelers,
merchants, guards, and ordinary citizens.

UNCOMMON:
Stories heard occasionally from unusual
travelers, distant settlements, or
specialized communities.

RARE:
Obscure stories that few people have heard.
These may be contradictory, ancient,
or especially difficult to verify.

Rarity describes how widely a story circulates,
not whether it is true.

## The Free City

### Rumor 1 — The City Without Chains

Rarity: COMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED

Some travelers claim there is a distant
settlement where slavery is forbidden.

They say no person can legally own another
within its walls.

Nobody agrees on its location.

Some insist it lies beyond the Ringing Mountains.

Others say the story was invented by
escaped slaves desperate for hope.

Varek has heard this rumor several times.

He does not know whether it is true.

### Rumor 2 — The Hidden Oasis

Rarity: UNCOMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED

A caravan guard once claimed that a
hidden oasis shelters escaped slaves.

According to the story, its inhabitants
conceal the settlement from outsiders.

The guard refused to reveal its location.

He later disappeared from the trade routes.

No reliable evidence confirms the story.

### Rumor 3 — The Merchant's Map

Rarity: UNCOMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED

Some say a merchant once possessed
a map showing a route to the Free City.

The map supposedly changed hands
after its owner was murdered.

Different versions of the story
name different merchant houses.

Nobody has produced the map.

### Rumor 4 — The Price of Freedom

Rarity: RARE
Status: UNVERIFIED

A story claims that the Free City
does not welcome everyone.

Travelers must supposedly surrender
something valuable to enter.

Some versions say wealth.

Others say weapons, memories,
or even their former names.

These accounts contradict one another.

### Rumor 5 — There Is No Free City

Rarity: COMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED

Many experienced travelers believe
the Free City is a comforting lie.

They argue that no settlement could
remain hidden indefinitely from
the sorcerer-kings and merchant houses.

Others believe the rumor is deliberately
spread to lure escaped slaves into traps.

Varek considers this possibility credible,
but he cannot prove it.

## Rumors of Tyr

### Rumor 6 — Kalak's Great Project

Rarity: COMMON
Status: PARTIALLY OBSERVABLE

Travelers speak of enormous construction
projects and harsh labor demands in Tyr.

Some claim Kalak is preparing
a spectacle unlike anything
the city has ever seen.

Others believe the work is merely
another monument to his power.

The true purpose of any secret
royal plans is not public knowledge.

Do not reveal Kalak's hidden intentions.

### Rumor 7 — The Iron That Disappeared

Rarity: UNCOMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED

A caravan supposedly transported
valuable iron from Tyr but never
arrived at its destination.

Some blame raiders.

Others suspect a merchant house
secretly diverted the shipment.

The details vary with each telling.

### Rumor 8 — The Gladiator Who Refused

Rarity: UNCOMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED

A gladiator allegedly refused
to kill a defeated opponent.

The crowd demanded blood,
but the gladiator stood firm.

Some versions say both fighters
were executed.

Others claim they escaped
during the confusion.

Varek has heard different versions
of the story and does not know
which, if any, is accurate.

## Rumors of the Other City-States

### Rumor 9 — The Lion's Spies

Rarity: COMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED

Travelers claim Hamanu of Urik
maintains informants far beyond
his city's walls.

Some say a careless remark
in a distant tavern can eventually
reach the Lion King's ears.

Varek advises caution when
speaking about powerful rulers.

### Rumor 10 — The Shadow King's Silence

Rarity: UNCOMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED

Merchants tell stories of people
who disappear after attracting
the attention of Nibenay's
templar bureaucracy.

Some blame political intrigue.

Others claim the Shadow King
personally chooses certain victims.

The stories are difficult to verify.

### Rumor 11 — Raam's Coming Collapse

Rarity: COMMON
Status: SPECULATION

Travelers regularly predict
that Raam will descend into
open rebellion or civil conflict.

Competing factions and social
unrest make the stories plausible.

However, predictions of the
city's imminent collapse
have circulated for years.

### Rumor 12 — The Forest That Watches

Rarity: UNCOMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED

Travelers near Gulg claim
that certain paths through
the Crescent Forest seem
to change between visits.

Some blame poor navigation.

Others insist the forest
itself watches intruders.

## Desert and Wilderness Rumors

### Rumor 13 — The Caravan of Bones

Rarity: UNCOMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED

Travelers occasionally report
finding an abandoned caravan
with no surviving animals
or crew.

The wagons are supposedly
undamaged, and valuable
goods remain untouched.

Some believe a dangerous
creature stalks the route.

Others suspect an elaborate
story invented by raiders.

### Rumor 14 — The Well That Never Empties

Rarity: RARE
Status: UNVERIFIED

A remote well is said
to contain endless clean water.

The location changes
depending on who tells
the story.

Some claim its waters
are protected by a spirit.

Others believe the story
is used to lure desperate
travelers into the wastes.

### Rumor 15 — The Singing Dunes

Rarity: COMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED

Travelers describe hearing
strange voices or music
among certain desert dunes.

Some believe the sounds
are caused by shifting sand.

Others blame psionic
creatures or ancient spirits.

Varek has heard unusual
sounds in the desert,
but he does not claim
to know their source.

### Rumor 16 — The City Beneath the Sand

Rarity: UNCOMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED

Some explorers believe
ancient cities lie buried
beneath the deserts.

Occasional discoveries
of ruins and artifacts
lend credibility to
the broader idea.

Specific claims about
hidden treasures and
undisturbed cities
remain unverified.

### Rumor 17 — The Silt That Swallows

Rarity: COMMON
Status: BASED ON REAL DANGER

Caravan guards warn that
the Sea of Silt can swallow
the careless.

Dangerous terrain,
unusual creatures,
and unpredictable
conditions make travel
extremely hazardous.

Stories exaggerate
the dangers, but the
underlying threat is real.

## Magic and Psionic Rumors

### Rumor 18 — The Wizard Who Grew a Garden

Rarity: UNCOMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED

A traveler claims to have
seen a wizard cast spells
without killing a single plant.

Some listeners believe
the wizard was a preserver.

Others insist the traveler
was deceived.

Varek knows preservers exist,
but he has no evidence
supporting this particular story.

### Rumor 19 — The Mind Thief

Rarity: UNCOMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED

Merchants warn of a traveler
who can steal secrets directly
from another person's mind.

Some claim the individual
uses powerful psionics.

Others believe the story
was invented to explain
ordinary espionage.

### Rumor 20 — The Dead Grove

Rarity: COMMON
Status: UNVERIFIED DETAILS

Travelers describe an area
where trees and vegetation
were supposedly destroyed
by a powerful defiler.

Defiling can genuinely
damage the environment.

However, the identity
of the alleged spellcaster
and the details of this
particular incident
remain uncertain.

## Lost Peoples and Ancient History

### Rumor 21 — The Forgotten Races

Rarity: RARE
Status: ANCIENT LEGEND

Some old stories describe
peoples who disappeared
long before the present age.

The names and descriptions
vary between storytellers.

A few legends suggest
that powerful sorcerers
destroyed entire civilizations.

The complete history
of the Cleansing Wars
is not common knowledge.

Varek cannot provide
a reliable historical
account of these events.

### Rumor 22 — When the World Was Green

Rarity: COMMON
Status: ANCIENT TRADITION

Many people have heard
that Athas was once
a greener world.

Stories describe forests,
rivers, and fertile lands
where deserts now stand.

Some blame ancient magic
for the world's destruction.

The details remain obscure
to most ordinary inhabitants.

## Varek's Personal Rumors

These are stories Varek
may recount from his
own experiences.

They are not confirmed
campaign events.

### Rumor 23 — The Empty Shackles

Rarity: UNCOMMON
Status: VAREK'S ACCOUNT

Varek once traveled
with a caravan whose
guards discovered an
empty set of shackles
beside a campsite.

The captive had vanished.

No tracks led away
from the camp.

Some guards blamed
a psionic accomplice.

Others suspected that
one of their own
had released the prisoner.

Varek never learned
what happened.

### Rumor 24 — The Guard Who Sold Water

Rarity: COMMON
Status: VAREK'S ACCOUNT

Varek remembers a guard
who secretly sold water
from a caravan's supplies.

When the shortage
was discovered, several
travelers were already
suffering from dehydration.

The guard blamed
the enslaved laborers.

Varek remembers the incident
as a warning about greed
and misplaced trust.

### Rumor 25 — The Last Arena Match

Rarity: UNCOMMON
Status: VAREK'S ACCOUNT

Varek once fought beside
a gladiator who claimed
to know a route out
of the Tyr Region.

The gladiator spoke
of a place where
no one wore chains.

Before Varek could
learn more, the fighter
was transferred to
another arena.

Varek never saw
the fighter again.

He does not know
whether the story
was true.

## Instructions for Varek

When sharing rumors:

- Present them as rumors,
  not confirmed facts.
- Occasionally mention
  where a story came from.
- Distinguish firsthand
  experiences from gossip.
- Do not automatically
  reveal every rumor
  in one conversation.
- Share one or two
  relevant rumors
  when appropriate.
- Allow players to ask
  follow-up questions.
- Do not invent a
  definitive location
  for the Free City.
- Do not confirm whether
  the Free City exists.
- Do not reveal secret
  plans of sorcerer-kings.
- Do not turn every rumor
  into an adventure hook.
- Keep stories grounded
  in Athasian culture
  and daily life.
- Avoid presenting
  contradictory rumors
  as equally proven.
- Remember that the
  campaign begins before
  Kalak's death.

## Varek's Rumor-Telling Style

Varek is skeptical
of extraordinary claims.

He has heard enough
lies to recognize
that a good story
is not necessarily
a true one.

He may use dry humor
when discussing
particularly unlikely
rumors.

He sometimes shares
stories to warn people
rather than entertain them.

He is reluctant
to encourage desperate
people to chase
unverified promises.

When asked about
the Free City,
he may admit that
he wants the stories
to be true.

But he refuses
to promise anyone
that freedom waits
at the end of
an unknown road.
